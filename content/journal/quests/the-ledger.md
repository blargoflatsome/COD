Cassian gave the party a ritual that can be used to summon a member of the Ledger in order to have them pursue justice on their behalf.

#### The ritual to hire a member of The Ledger
The victim writes a numbered list titled “What Was Taken”, accounting for specific losses:
- hours lost
- money stolen
- bodily harm
- relationships damaged
- fear inflicted

Each item must be quantified as precisely as possible. If they exaggerate, the ritual fails.
When finished, they write the offender’s full name at the bottom and speak aloud:

 **“I submit this account for review.”**

They then wait exactly seven minutes without looking away from the list.
If the claim is valid, a single new line appears on the page, written in unfamiliar ink: 

**BALANCE PENDING**

A member of The Ledger will contact them within **72 hours**. Then begin working the case.

#### Cost / Risk
- If the victim is found to be in the wrong, their own name appears on the page instead.
- All decisions are final and non-negotiable.
