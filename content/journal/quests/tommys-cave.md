Eowyn, to gain access to the Catacombs from Luca Klein, agreed to seek out two missing members of the Klein family. Asha and Tommy Klein. 
- Asha was discovered having apparently starved to death. The party found her journal and stole it from the crime scene.
- Her journal mentions seeing Tommy inside a cave.
- The Party has found Tommy Klein inside a cave where is hiding from Father.
- Bob destroyed the Shrine the goblins had built to keep Tommy safe (Party Goal: Destroy something sacred)
- They defeated the Father and Tommy returned to being a normal child. The cave collapsed as they left.
