The party now knows that Silas Hale, the ex-cult leader being pursued by Tofu, is in the Catacombs. The psionic rats that attacked Cassian's castle were servants of Silas and carried a religious symbol to his new religion: The Held. 
- Tami discovered that there is a city named The City of Refuge that is home to 12 competing faiths. Among them is The Held.
- The Held are giving out "miracle cure" communion wafers made of human skin and cursed lake water to treat those with the plague.
  - Beef learned that the wafers also infect anyone who takes them with the plague.
