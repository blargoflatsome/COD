The party gathered at the South Gate of the City of Refuge. While they were still working out how they all knew each other, the gates opened and Tessaly Rook walked out of the city. Bernard ducked behind a nearby crypt until she was gone. Rook looked the Orc, Brick, over carefully as she passed, but she kept walking and said nothing.

Inspectors at the gate were checking every hand before letting anyone through, and the party was three places from the front when Bernard brought up an abandoned church. They left the line and went to see it instead.

Behind a secret door in the church, a passage led down to Archon Pell. While they were there, Beef joined the Ossified. With Pell's help she studied the communion wafer, and her research went better than anyone could have hoped. When the party mentioned the dead leaving the cave, Pell said he knew nothing about it. Tami had her doubts about that, but she kept them to herself.

The party walked three hours from the church to the cave. As they arrived, five undead were dragging two living captives toward the entrance. The party attacked and destroyed four of them. The fifth got away into the dark of the cave.

Both captives had red fingernails. Beef wanted to know why, so she cut flesh from one of the men while he was still alive and then slit his throat. His blood got on her, and soon after, her own fingernails began to turn red.

Tami knelt over the other captive and prayed. Her god answered with a vision: she was to spread his word in this place and bring its people to his worship. When the vision ended, the man's fingernails were no longer red.

As the session ended, the party was getting ready to go into the cave after the undead that escaped.
