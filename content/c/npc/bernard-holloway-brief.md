> *"I just want to be brave."*

## Who You Are

**Bernard Holloway, 34.** Back in the real world you were an office administrator and a chronic appeaser: the guy who books the conference room, apologizes when someone else bumps into him, and stays late so nobody's mad at him.

You paid to cross into the Catacombs on the same day as Tami and Beef. You heard the place makes you who you really are. It made you **a rat-man.** Whiskers, twitching nose, claws, and a tail you forget about until it knocks something over.

You never came out where they did. While they ended up in a place called Gear City, you woke up alone in **a cave outside the City of Refuge**, already a ratman.

You tried to get into the city once. A member of the Held, in red with a gold crown pin, was out hunting Howlers. She took one look at you, decided you were one, and chased you all the way back to your cave. You've lived there ever since. At night people hunt you, because they think your crying is a monster howling. It's not howling. You just cry. A lot.

You also really, *really* want some cheese.

## How to Play Him

- **Apologize first, talk second.** "Sorry, sorry, I don't mean to interrupt, it's just..."
- **You over-explain** when you're nervous, which is always.
- **You notice everything.** Fear keeps you watching, so you know exactly who walked past, when, and which way they went.
- **You write things up.** You've kept a little notebook of "incident reports" about everything you've seen out here. You once got an office bully fired with a carefully worded email sent at 5pm on a Friday. Paperwork is your weapon.
- **You hate confrontation.** When someone challenges you, your voice shakes.
- **Hood up, always.** Your face makes people afraid of you, which is the cruelest joke, because you're afraid of everyone.

### What You Want (Hopes)

1. To speak without your voice shaking when someone challenges you.
2. To matter in a moment of crisis, not just be there.
3. To look back on one choice and feel proud instead of ashamed.

### What You're Afraid Of

1. That bravery is something you're born with, and you just weren't.
2. That people only tolerate you because you're useful and quiet.
3. That when it matters most, you'll freeze again.

### What Haunts You

- You didn't stop your father from humiliating your mother in public. You looked away.
- You let someone else take the blame for a mistake that was yours. They got fired.
- You did report harassment at work, but everyone called you a narc for it.

## Mechanics

- **Disguise.** Keep your face hidden with a heavy hood or *Disguise Self*. While your rat features are visible, you have **disadvantage on Charisma checks.**
- **Freeze or push.** When something scary happens, freezing is completely in character and totally fine to play. When Bernard pushes through something that really terrifies him, make a big deal of it. Those are the moments that count toward his Hopes.
- **You can talk to rats** (*Speak with Animals*, as a ritual). The rats outside the walls are your friends, and some of what's in your incident log came from them.
- **Your perfume** (Perfume of Bewitching, one use) makes people warm up to you for an hour, even with the hood down. Save it for when it really counts.
- **Bardic Inspiration** (twice per long rest) is how you help even when you're too scared to act yourself.
- **Brave.** You have advantage on saves against being Frightened. Bernard doesn't believe it, but it's on your sheet.

## The People You're About to Meet

- **Tami.** A human paladin. You watched her YouTube channel back home, which is how you knew her name before you ever met. She's sort of famous to you.
- **Beef.** An elf wizard who was a veterinarian in the real world.
- **Brick.** A big orc monk you've never met. People stare at him too.

They won't recognize you. The last time they saw you, you were a regular guy in a cardigan at the back of the group. Tami looks the same, and so does Beef, apart from her ears.

## Your Incident Log

These are things you saw. You don't know what they mean, and you shouldn't guess too hard. Just report them, nervously and in too much detail.

1. **The night carts.** Volunteers in red with gold crown pins come out to the sick camps outside the walls after dark. They load up the dead and say they're taking them "to the pyres." They never go near the pyres. They go in through a small side gate where nobody checks anyone's hands.
2. **The wafers.** Those same volunteers hand out thin red wafers in the camps. People who stopped getting them got sick fast, even a man who was healthy when he started and only took them "to be safe."
3. **The howling.** You know the real Howlers from your own crying. Theirs sounds like several voices whispering at once.
4. **The graveyard.** You sneak in at night to pick through the offerings left on graves for food, because nobody comes after dark. You've seen a fresh grave sunken in by morning, and once you heard voices *near an old abandoned church.* You would have tried to stay there, but it gave you the creeps worse than your cave. A few nights ago, though, someone started leaving bread out at night.
5. **Others in the cave.** You've seen people shuffle out of the cave from somewhere deeper inside. They smell of death, but you've managed to stay out of sight. They aren't Howlers: they talk to each other and keep watch like soldiers. Sometimes they come back empty-handed, and sometimes they come back dragging screaming people.
6. **The grey hoods.** There's a camp of grey-hooded people from the city who burn the bodies of the dead. They don't seem to get along with the red robes, but you keep your distance from both.

## Your Goals This Session

1. **Find Tami and Beef and get them to help you.** You need someone to speak for you, get you inside the walls, and prove you're not a threat.
2. **Tell them what you've seen.** Your incident log is the most useful thing you've ever written. Get at least a few of these entries in front of the party.
3. **Survive the people who hate you.** The red-robed Held are telling the city you *brought* the plague. You need to be cleared of that.
4. **Have one brave moment.** Just one. Speak without your voice shaking, step in front of someone, or make a choice you'll be proud of. When it happens, you'll know.
5. **Stick with the party.** Wherever they go, you go, even if every part of you wants to run back to your cave.
