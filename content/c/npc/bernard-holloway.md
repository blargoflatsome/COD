Bernard Holloway is a Traveler, a man in his mid 30's who crossed the Threshold with the party but did not arrive in Gear City with them. He is now a ratman, desperate to be brave.

## Character Relations
He found Tami and Beef outside the City of Refuge. At the South Gate, when Tessaly Rook walked out of the city, he ducked behind a nearby crypt until she was gone.
