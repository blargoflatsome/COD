Archon Pell belongs to the Ossified, one of the twelve faiths of the City of Refuge. The party found him behind a secret door in an abandoned church, down a hidden passage.

The [survey of Refuge](/c/lore/city-of-refuge-lore) lists an Archon Pell Vayne as the Ossified's representative on the Inter-Faith Council.

## Character Relations
While the party was with him, Beef joined the Ossified. With his help she studied the communion wafer The Held hand out to the sick, and her research went better than anyone could have hoped.

When the party mentioned the dead leaving the cave, Pell said he knew nothing about it. Tami has her doubts.
