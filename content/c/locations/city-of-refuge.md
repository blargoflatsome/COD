The city of Refuge is more ruin than city with billowing pyre smoke chocking the air and coating the lands in soot. The graveyard bordering the lake is littered in half-dug graves, overturned caskets, and robed clergy shouting over one another as they attempt to hold competing service for the dead. 

Twelve faiths all call the lake near the city sacred and compete over their devotion and ruling of the city. Among them is The Held, the new religion of Silas Hale.

A plague called the Red Benediction has taken hold here. The Held hand out "miracle cure" in the form of thin red communion wafers. However, Beef learned they are made of human skin and cursed lake water. She also discovered that they infect anyone who takes them with the very plague they are meant to cure.

## The Red Benediction

<img src="/images/red-benediction-stages.webp" alt="The four stages of the Red Benediction: Marked, Exposed, Infectious and Final" width="1376" height="768" class="w-full h-auto rounded-lg border mb-4" />

The plague moves through four stages.

1. **Marked**: the fingernails turn red.
2. **Exposed**: dark veins spread up the arms from the hands.
3. **Infectious**: the sick begin to bleed from the eyes and mouth.
4. **Final**: the eyes fill with blood, which runs down the face like tears.
